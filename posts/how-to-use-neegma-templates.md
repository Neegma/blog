Every host knows the feeling. The calendar invite is for tomorrow, your team or your guests are expecting something good, and you don't have three hours to write trivia questions, format a deck, or test slide animations.

That's exactly why [Neegma Templates](https://tryneegma.com/templates) exist.

Instead of starting from a blank screen, templates give you a curated, structured game session built for a specific kind of occasion, from corporate offsites and remote team check-ins to birthday game nights, community mixers, and pub-style trivia.

## What is a Neegma template?

A template is a session you've already put together (or someone else has), ready to open and host immediately. Each one bundles together:

- **Curated games.** Formats like Unscramble, Taboo, Missing Letter, Bingo, Find the Mole, or Two Truths & a Lie, picked to match the vibe of the event.
- **Content that's actually thought through.** Prompts, categories, and question decks suited to the group.
- **Pacing and timing already set.** Round counts and timers configured so you're not guessing how long anything will take.

Guests still join the exact same way, no matter which template you pick: scan a QR code or tap a link on their phone, no app download, no account.

## Templates built for real moments

Different rooms need different energy. A five person family dinner doesn't want the same format as an 80 person company town hall.

### 1. Team offsites and work socials

When you're bringing colleagues together, the goal is getting people talking without the usual corporate stiffness. Work focused templates pair a low pressure opener like Two Truths & a Lie with a faster, collaborative game like Taboo in Team Mode, where tables huddle and argue over answers together.

### 2. Community mixers and gatherings

For community managers running workshops or meetups, templates built around Social Bingo or Unscramble get attendees interacting within the first five minutes, turning a quiet, watchful arrival into an engaged room before the keynote even starts.

### 3. Birthday parties and game nights

For casual celebrations, party templates lean into competitive, lively formats like Word Art, Song Association, and Charades.
