If you create trivia packs, icebreaker cards, party games, or facilitator workshops, you already know the frustration of the current creator toolkit.

You spend hours researching great questions and designing a beautiful printable PDF or slide deck. Then a buyer downloads the file, and the magic gets lost in execution: someone has to print paper sheets, track scores on a whiteboard, and wrestle with a phone timer propped against a glass.

The [Neegma Partner Program](https://tryneegma.com/use-cases/sellers) exists so creators can package and sell their games as live, interactive game shows instead.

## What is the Neegma Partner Program?

It's built for creators, event hosts, team facilitators, and creative agencies who design game experiences for a living.

Instead of selling a static printout or a slide deck, you build your game content in the Playground and, once you're an approved partner, publish it as a template on Neegma. Customers get a link that launches a fully hosted, interactive game:

- Players join live on their phones by QR code or link.
- No player downloads or accounts required.
- Real-time scoring, live leaderboards, and automatic timers handle the technical side, so you don't have to.

Becoming a partner is an application rather than an instant switch. You apply through [Neegma Partners](https://tryneegma.com/partners), the team reviews it, and once approved you get access to build a branded storefront at your own `/partner/` link.

## Why event creators are partnering with Neegma

### 1. Sell an experience, not homework

A printable PDF asks your customer to do the work. A Neegma powered game lets them click host, cast the screen to a TV, and immediately look like a professional game show host with none of the manual setup.

### 2. Custom branding that showcases your work

Partners can theme their storefront and sessions with their own logo and colour palette, so your brand stays front and centre while Neegma runs the multiplayer engine underneath.

### 3. A catalogue allowance that comes with the role

Approved partners get a higher allowance for custom games and templates than a standard plan, and it applies no matter which plan you're on. You don't need to be paying for Pro just to start building your catalogue.

You can see what a live partner storefront actually looks like at [tryneegma.com/partner/taog](https://tryneegma.com/partner/taog).

## How to turn your games into a live experience

1. **Build in the Playground.** Add your questions, custom logic, and clues, or import your full deck by CSV.
2. **Apply to the Partner Program.** Once you're approved, package your content into a template from the games you've already built.
3. **List it for sale.** Share it directly with your own audience, or list it inside the Neegma template catalogue for new customers to find.

Whether you run an agency producing large scale corporate activations or you write boutique dinner party games on the side, this gives your audience a genuinely modern way to play them.
