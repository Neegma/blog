Every printable party game, trivia pack, or bachelorette scavenger hunt started the same way: as a set of good questions or a clever format, turned into a nicely designed PDF. If you're already selling those on Etsy, eBay, Teachers Pay Teachers, or through your own Canva templates, you've done the hard part. You wrote content people actually want to play.

The part still stuck in 2015 is what happens after someone buys it. They download a PDF, print it at home or at a shop, cut the cards apart, and hope nothing curls coming out of the printer. Then somebody still has to read the questions out loud, keep score on a napkin, and watch a phone for the timer.

[Neegma](https://tryneegma.com) exists so the second half of that experience can be as good as the first. The [Partner Program](https://tryneegma.com/use-cases/sellers) lets you turn the same content you're already selling as a printable into a fully hosted, live game show. No printer, no scissors, no napkin scoreboard.

## What changes when you sell printable party games as a live show

Your product doesn't change. A trivia pack is still a trivia pack, a bachelorette scavenger hunt is still a scavenger hunt, a baby shower bingo set is still bingo. What changes is what the buyer gets: instead of, or alongside, a PDF, they get a link. They open it on a laptop, cast it to a TV or share their screen, and their guests join by scanning a QR code on their own phones. Nobody but the host needs an app or an account.

- Players join live on their phones by QR code or link.
- Scoring, timers, and a live leaderboard are handled for you.
- Real results replace a printed answer key nobody can read from across the table.

## Why Etsy, Canva, and TPT sellers are adding this

### It sells the same content twice

A printable and a hosted version aren't competing with each other. List both. Buyers who want a physical keepsake still get the PDF. Buyers who just want the party to run itself get the live link instead.

### No more printer roulette

A card that doesn't cut straight, a colour that doesn't print true, text too small to read from across the room: none of it exists once the product is a screen instead of a sheet of paper. What you designed looks exactly as good on game night as it did in your listing photos.

### Your brand still leads

Approved partners can theme their storefront and sessions with their own logo and colour palette, so a customer who found you through your Etsy shop or your Canva templates still recognises your work the moment they open the live game.

### The catalogue allowance comes with the role, not a subscription

You don't need to be paying for a premium plan to start building. The partner allowance for custom games and templates is higher than any standard plan's, and it applies no matter which plan you're already on.

1. **Rebuild your questions in the Playground.** If you've already written a trivia deck or a set of prompts for a printable, most of that work carries straight over, you're re-entering content, not reinventing it. CSV import handles it in one go if your questions already live in a spreadsheet.
2. **Apply to the Partner Program.** Applications go through [Neegma Partners](https://tryneegma.com/partners) and are reviewed before you're approved.
3. **Package it as a template and list it.** Once approved, turn the games you've built into a template customers can buy and host themselves, alongside your printable or instead of it.
