Big brand activations are one kind of challenge. A birthday party with 20 guests, half of whom have never met, is a different one entirely. There is no stage, no emcee working a script, just a room that needs a reason to start talking to itself. On 11 July, that was exactly the task facing the host of this birthday celebration, and Neegma was brought in to give the room that reason.

## Breaking the Ice with Find the Mole

The birthday girl had already been trying to get her guests mixing, but conversation only goes so far without a shared task to hang it on. So the group turned to [Find the Mole](https://tryneegma.com/game/find-the-mole), the social deduction game where everyone in the room gets a category and a secret word, except for one person, the mole, who has to bluff their way through without knowing what the word actually is.

![Guests choosing the next game on a tablet at the birthday party](https://res.cloudinary.com/dzpntisxj/image/upload/v1784363682/Neegma/events/choose_choose_game_ofjzrc.jpg)

About 18 guests joined the round by phone, and the format did exactly what it was brought in to do. Suddenly, everyone had to explain themselves. Why did you say that word? What made you think of that? Every answer became a small performance, and every performance was being quietly judged by the rest of the table.

## The Word Was "Thin." The Answer Was "Pen."

Every game like this needs one moment that becomes the story people retell afterwards, and this one arrived fast. The category was stationery, and the secret word was "thin". One of the moles, who did not have the word and was trying to guess their way through the round, offered "pen" as their related answer.

It was close, but it was not close enough, and the room caught it instantly. Nothing gives away a mole faster than an almost right guess.

Not every round ended that cleanly, though. Across four rounds, one mole managed to survive an entire round undetected, reading the other players' clues closely enough to fake familiarity with a word they had never seen. The room was convinced they had them, right up until the reveal proved otherwise.

## Rolling Into Charades

With the room fully warmed up, the group moved into two rounds of [Charades](https://tryneegma.com/game/charades), the classic mime-and-guess game, hosted straight from one device. The energy that Find the Mole had built carried straight over. Guests who had spent the last twenty minutes bluffing their way through a deduction game were now acting out words in front of everyone, and the mix of confident performers and hilariously bad mimes kept the laughter constant for both rounds.

You could feel the shift happening. People who had walked in as strangers were now shouting guesses at each other like they had known each other for years. That is the whole point of putting a game in the middle of a party. It gives people permission to be loud with each other.

## A Small Party That Played Big

What made the evening work was not the size of the crowd; it was how completely the games closed the gap between people who arrived not knowing each other. By the time the structured games wound down, the room had shared jokes, shared near-misses, and shared enough laughter that the rest of the night took care of itself.

Whether it is a milestone birthday or an intimate get-together, a well-placed deduction game and a couple of rounds of Charades can do more for the room than another hour of standing around with a drink.
