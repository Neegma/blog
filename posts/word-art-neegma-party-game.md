Most party word games test how fast you can type. But the games that create the biggest laughs around a table, or in a crowded lounge, are the ones where people have to think on their feet, shout their answer out loud, and defend it to a room full of friends who get to vote on whether it counts.

That's the whole premise of [Word Art](https://tryneegma.com/game/word-art), one of the newest games in the Neegma library. Here's the exact screen a player sees mid-round.

## How Word Art works

Word Art is a host led game for 2 to 50 individual players, or up to 10 teams, playable from a single shared screen in person or spread across phones and video calls.

1. **The host reveals a prompt.** A word rule appears on every screen at once, along with whose turn it is. It might be "starts with S and contains R," "a compound word," or "name a food that starts with a vowel."
2. **The team beats the clock.** The player or team whose turn it is has 15, 20, or 30 seconds (the host picks the pace) to say a real word out loud that fits the rule.
3. **The room judges, the host decides.** There's no typing and no automatic checking. The room hears the answer and judges it together, and the host taps Got It or Missed.
4. **A hit is worth 1000 points.** A clean answer inside the countdown scores the full 1000 and play rotates to the next team.
5. **A miss opens the steal.** If nobody lands a valid word in time, the next team in rotation gets a five second window to steal it for 500 points, since they already heard the prompt while they were waiting their turn. Miss the steal too, and nobody scores, but the game keeps moving.

## Why Word Art sparks instant room energy

### Low barrier, genuinely high engagement

There's no typing, no complicated setup, and nothing to read except the prompt. The moment it appears, everyone in the room is already thinking of their own answer, which is exactly why players who aren't up yet stay locked in rather than checking out until their turn comes round.

### The steal keeps everyone alert

Because a missed prompt hands the next team a real shot at points, nobody gets to relax once their own turn ends. You're listening for the prompt, planning a backup word, and watching for your chance the entire time, not just during your own thirty seconds.

### Works from a sofa to a full team-mode crowd

Playing solo turn by turn works beautifully for a small group of 4 to 10 people. Switch on Team Mode for anything bigger, let tables huddle, argue over the best word, and appoint a speaker before opponents get a chance to jump in on the steal.

### Playable online or in the room

Cast the host screen to a living room TV or conference projector, or share your screen on Zoom, Teams, or Google Meet. Online, players see the prompt land on their own phone the instant it's revealed. Offline, there's no phones needed at all, just the host's screen and the room's ears.

If you're looking for an opener that gets an energising laugh out of a team social, birthday hangout, or after-work drinks, Word Art delivers immediate back and forth with almost no explanation required.
